# PrismatiX logo

`PrismatiXEngine_Logo.png` (1024x320) is the PrismatiX Engine logo, copied
from the engine's `Resources/Branding` for the startup screen
`Content/UI/Splash.tsx`, where it is shown on a black background.

You may show it unmodified in a game made with PrismatiX. You may also
replace the startup screen with your own logo, or remove it: delete the
`player.splashes` entry (Game flow, or Project settings > Startup screens in
PrismatiX Studio) and, if you like, the `splash` route and this folder. Do not
alter the logo or use it to suggest that the PrismatiX project endorses your
game.
