# PrismatiX logo

`prismatix-mark.png` (960x880) and `prismatix-wordmark.png` (1096x203) are the
PrismatiX brand mark, the faceted prism, and its wordmark, set in Geist
(SIL Open Font License 1.1). Both were rendered from the vector mark that
PrismatiX Studio ships (`public/prismatix.svg`) and are distributed with the
PrismatiX starter for the "Made with PrismatiX" startup screen,
`Content/UI/Splash.tsx`.

You may show them unmodified in a game made with PrismatiX. You may also
replace the startup screen with your own logo, or remove it: delete the
`player.splashes` entry (Game flow, or Project settings > Startup screens in
PrismatiX Studio) and, if you like, the `splash` route and these files. Do not
alter the mark or use it to suggest that the PrismatiX project endorses your
game.
